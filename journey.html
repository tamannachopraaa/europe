Switzerland Zurich

Switzerland - Zurich

Zurich is a beautiful city with a perfect blend of nature and urban life. The views from Uetliberg Mountain are breathtaking, and the Old Town is full of charm. Can't wait to explore more!

Switzerland Bern

Switzerland-Bern

The history in Bern is palpable. Walking along the Zytglogge and seeing the Bear Park was surreal. The city has such a cool, creative energy (and the best chocolate).

Switzerland Zurich

Switzerland- Lindt Chocolate Factory

Visited the Lindt Chocolate Factory in Zurich. The aroma of chocolate was everywhere! Learned about the chocolate-making process and even got to taste some delicious samples.

Switzerland Interlaken

Switzerland-Interlaken

Interlaken is a paradise for adventure seekers. Surrounded by mountains, it's the perfect base for hiking, skiing, and exploring the Swiss Alps. The views are absolutely breathtaking!

Italy Venice

Italy- Venice

Venice is a city like no other. The canals, the gondolas, the stunning architecture... it's all so romantic. Getting lost in the narrow streets was one of the highlights of my trip.

Italy Rome

Italy - Rome

Rome is a city steeped in history. From the ancient ruins of the Roman Forum to the stunning architecture of the Vatican, every corner is a reminder of its glorious past. Enjoyed authentic Roman pizza in Trastevere!

Germany Konstanz

Germany - Konstanz

Konstanz is a charming town on the shores of Lake Constance. Loved strolling through the old town and enjoying the lakeside views. The seafood here is incredible!

France Paris

France - Paris

Exploring the City of Light was a dream come true. From the iconic Eiffel Tower to the charming streets of Montmartre, every moment was magical. Indulged in delicious pastries and soaked in the art at the Louvre.

France Paris

Disneyland Paris

Disneyland Paris was a dream come true. From meeting Mickey Mouse to experiencing the magic of the castle, every moment was filled with joy. The fireworks show was the perfect ending to an unforgettable trip.

Netherlands Amsterdam

Netherlands-Amsterdam

Amsterdam was a delightful experience. The canals, the bikes, and the vibrant culture made it unforgettable. Visited the Van Gogh Museum and took a scenic boat tour.
